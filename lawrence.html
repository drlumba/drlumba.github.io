Background

Originally from Vancouver, BC, Dr. Lawrence Lumba completed his undergraduate degree in Kinesiology at the University of British Columbia before earning his Doctor of Chiropractic at the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College. That foundation in movement science shapes his approach today — care built around function, recovery, and helping patients get back to what they enjoy.
