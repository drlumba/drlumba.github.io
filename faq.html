Frequently Asked Questions.

Answers to common questions about chiropractic care, booking, insurance, and what to expect when you visit Dr. Lawrence Lumba or Dr. Alex Tremblett in Windsor and Tecumseh.

Getting Started

Do I need a doctor's referral to see a chiropractor?

No referral is needed in Ontario. You can book directly with Dr. Lawrence Lumba or Dr. Alex Tremblett online, and many extended health insurance plans cover chiropractic care without a physician referral. Check your specific plan for details.

What happens at my first chiropractic visit?

Your first visit includes a discussion of your health history and goals, a movement and physical assessment, and a treatment plan tailored to your condition. Most patients also receive treatment during the initial visit, depending on assessment findings.

Does chiropractic care hurt?

Chiropractic treatment is generally not painful. Some techniques can cause brief mild discomfort or soreness afterward, similar to what you might feel after exercise, but this typically resolves within a day or two.

How many chiropractic visits will I need?

The number of visits varies by individual, condition, and goals. After your initial assessment, your chiropractor will discuss a recommended treatment plan and expected timeline with you.

Conditions We Help With

Can a chiropractor help with lower back pain?

Yes. Chiropractic care is commonly used to help manage lower back pain, stiffness, and recurring flare-ups through a combination of hands-on treatment, mobility work, and exercise-based rehabilitation.

Can a chiropractor help with neck pain and headaches?

Chiropractic treatment can help address neck tension, stiffness, and headaches related to posture or muscular strain, particularly issues linked to prolonged desk work or screen use.

What is the GLA:D program for osteoarthritis?

GLA:D (Good Life with osteoArthritis in Denmark) is an evidence-based education and exercise program for people with hip and knee osteoarthritis. Dr. Alex Tremblett offers guidance using GLA:D principles to help patients manage symptoms and improve function.

Do you treat sports injuries?

Yes. Dr. Lawrence Lumba works with patients recovering from training, sport, and gym-related injuries, combining chiropractic care with rehabilitation strategies to support a safe return to activity.

Is chiropractic care covered by insurance?

Many extended health plans in Ontario include chiropractic coverage. Coverage amounts vary by provider and plan, so we recommend checking with your insurer before your visit. Receipts are provided for reimbursement.
