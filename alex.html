Background

Care Close to Home

Dr. Alex Tremblett grew up in Tecumseh, Ontario and studied Human Kinetics at the University of Windsor before completing her Doctor of Chiropractic at the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College. Her patient-centred approach focuses on mobility, active aging, and helping people move confidently at every stage of life.

Clinical Interests

Hip & Knee Osteoarthritis

Education and exercise strategies for people living with hip and knee osteoarthritis. Ask Alex about GLA:D-based care and current program availability.

Active Aging

Supporting mobility, independence, and participation in everyday activities.

Movement Confidence

Guidance as you return to exercise, recreation, work, and daily life.
