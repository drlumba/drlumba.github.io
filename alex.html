Background

Dr. Alex Lumba grew up in Tecumseh, ON and studied Human Kinetics at the University of Windsor before completing his Doctor of Chiropractic at the Canadian Memorial Chiropractic College. Now practicing close to home, she brings a patient-centered approach focused on mobility, active aging, and helping people move confidently at every stage of life.
